Foldcount

How it works

Foldcount counts how often you open your iPhone Duo and how long it stays open. Two Shortcuts automations you set up once (“Opened” and “Closed”) tell Foldcount about every fold, even while the app is closed. The app shows today, your last 7 days, records and milestones; Foldcount Pro unlocks your full history, all-time records, the heatmap, CSV export and weekly share cards.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up the automations?

Open Shortcuts → Automation → +. Under Display, choose “Opened or Closed” → Add. Choose “Opened”, leave Automation on and turn Notify off, then search for Foldcount and add “Log Unfold”. Repeat with “Closed” and “Log Fold”. The app walks you through it and runs a short test.

Foldcount stopped counting. What can I do?

Check in Shortcuts that both automations are still on and set to run without asking. In Foldcount, Settings → Set up automations again runs the live test.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I get it back?

Open the Pro screen and tap “Restore purchases”. Pro is shared with your Family Sharing group.

Does Foldcount work offline?

Yes. No internet connection is needed.

Does Foldcount collect my data?

No. See our Privacy Policy for details.
